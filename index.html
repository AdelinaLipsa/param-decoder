The four modes

✓

Inspect

Read and repair one link. Every param is labelled in plain English; flags are red / amber / green; edit values inline, toggle params off, then copy a clean rebuilt link.

Compare

Two links side by side, merged by param. Differences in aff_id and subid stand out the most — the fastest way to answer "why does one track and the other doesn't".

Batch

Paste a whole spreadsheet column of links, get a green/red pass-fail table back. Check a campaign's worth of links at once; jump into any row with one click.

Postback

Check a tracker's postback URL against the five tokens BuyGoods actually fills ({SUBID}…{SUBID5}, {ORDERID}, {COMMISSION_AMOUNT}…). Catches tokens BuyGoods will never send.

What the flags mean

Error — the link is broken. Usually a missing or empty aff_id: no commission will attribute.
Warning — worth a look, won't block. Duplicate params, a utm_source with spaces, an incomplete UTM set.
OK — recognised and valid. Unknown offer params default here; {PLACEHOLDER} tokens are always fine.
Heads-up — aff_id pays the affiliate; a subid click-ID is what reports the conversion back to a tracker. They're different jobs.

Saved names — stored on this device only, never sent
