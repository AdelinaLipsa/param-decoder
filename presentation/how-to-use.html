● Param Decoder2

Mode · Inspect

Read & repair one link.

Paste any offer or checkout URL, even messy, encoded, or with a Base64 redirect, and you get a labelled table back, one row per param:

aff_id  (empty)  affiliate earns no commission · one-click fix offered
subid2  clk_abc123  click ID · tracker will credit the sale
utm_source  unrecognised param · did you mean utm_source?

Fix the red flag inline, then copy the clean rebuilt link, or the ticket-ready diagnosis, straight into the ticket.

● Param Decoder4

Mode · Postback

Set up a tracker postback right.

A postback is a URL BuyGoods fires when a sale completes, it’s how the affiliate’s tracker learns a conversion happened. If the tokens are wrong, the tracker records nothing, even though the sale went through. Paste their postback URL; the tool flags which tokens are present, missing, or wrong, then copy the corrected template:

Voluum: cid = click ID, payout = revenue, txid = order ID.
CPV Lab: subid = click ID, revenue = payout.
AnyTrack: click_id, value, transactionId.

Each template is sourced from the tracker’s own docs, with a copy button.

● Param Decoder5

Mode · Decline

Explain a failed payment.

A decline code comes from the transaction, not a URL. In BuyGoods, the transaction record shows the raw code. Paste exactly what appears there:

→ do_not_honor  (Braintree 2000)
type:   hard, permanent for this card
action: Ask the customer to contact their bank or use a different card. Don’t retry.
Soft

Temporary

A retry can still work, insufficient funds, issuer busy.

Hard

Permanent for this card

Expired, lost / stolen, do-not-honor, ask for another card.

● Param Decoder6

Reading the screen

What the colours & badges mean.

Status flags

Error, broken; usually a missing aff_id or unfilled token. Fix before it goes live.
Warning, present but suspicious: a UTM with spaces, a leftover template variable.
OK, recognised and valid; nothing to do.
Heads-up, info: this param carries the click ID, not commission.

Trust badges, who vouches for the meaning

confirmed
Set by the team, rely on it.
inferred
The tool’s best guess, treat as provisional.
noted
You saved this meaning on this device.
