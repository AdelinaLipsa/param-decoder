● Param Decoder2

What it is

A parameter utility — nothing more, on purpose.

It reads, validates, fixes, and compares BuyGoods URLs, checks tracker postbacks, and explains decline codes. Analytics and reporting stay in the company’s own clickcrm.com platform — this tool doesn’t duplicate them.

So a junior can paste a link and get the right answer without a veteran AM.
And paste live customer / order links safely — it never phones home.
● Param Decoder3

The moat

It knows BuyGoods — the two jobs of every link.

A single click does two separate jobs. Telling them apart is the whole point.

aff_idsubid
Commission — aff_id tells BuyGoods which affiliate to pay.
Reporting — the click ID in a subid lets the affiliate’s tracker credit the sale.

A link can pay the affiliate yet never report back — or track perfectly yet earn no commission. Different breakage, different fix.

● Param Decoder7

Never bluffs

A living dictionary the team grows.

Every param carries a trust level, so an AM always knows what to rely on:

confirmed
Shipped, team-trusted — no badge in the table.
inferred
A best guess from real links — flagged as not-yet-verified.
noted
A meaning you captured on this device — it then explains itself on every link.

Describe an unknown param or confirm an inferred one, and the dictionary gets smarter — without a deploy.
